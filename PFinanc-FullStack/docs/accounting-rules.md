# Accounting & Financial Principles Specification — PFinanc

This document outlines the strict financial principles, invariants, and mathematical algorithms implemented in PFinanc to guarantee auditability, ledger correctness, zero double-counting, and accurate investment portfolio accounting.

---

## 1. Core Invariants

1. **Single Source of Truth**: Financial balances are derived deterministically from the canonical transaction and investment ledgers.
2. **Investment Purchase Is NOT an Expense**:
   > When a user purchases an investment asset (Stock, Mutual Fund, ETF), funds are reallocated from a Cash/Bank account to an Investment Position. This cash movement must **never** be classified as a household expense or distort monthly cash-flow burn rates.
3. **Transfer Isolation**: Internal family transfers between household accounts must NEVER alter overall household net worth or inflate income/expense metrics.
4. **Draft Safety**: Unconfirmed drafts generated by automated ingestion (SMS, Email, CSV) do not alter financial balances or portfolio valuations until confirmed.
5. **Void Immutability**: Historical records are never deleted; instead, they are transitioned to `VOID` status, preserving a complete audit trail.
6. **Exact Decimal Precision**: All monetary values use `NUMERIC(18,2)`, price per share/NAV uses `NUMERIC(18,4)`, and holding quantities use `NUMERIC(24,8)` to support fractional mutual fund units without IEEE-754 precision loss.

---

## 2. Integrated Net Worth Derivation

Household Net Worth at any moment is calculated as:

$$\text{Net Worth} = \text{Liquid Cash Assets} + \text{Investment Market Value} + \text{Fixed Income} + \text{Retirement Corpus} - \text{Total Liabilities}$$

Where:
* **Liquid Cash Assets**: $\sum (\text{Bank} + \text{Cash} + \text{Wallet Balances})$
* **Investment Market Value**: $\sum_{\text{Securities}} (\text{Current Quantity} \times \text{Latest Valid Price / NAV})$
* **Fixed Income**: $\sum \text{Current Accrued Value of Active Fixed Deposits}$
* **Retirement Corpus**: $\sum \text{Current Balances of EPF, PPF, and NPS Accounts}$
* **Total Liabilities**: $\sum (\text{Credit Card Balances} + \text{Active Loans})$

## 3. FIFO Cost-Basis & Realized / Unrealized P&L

To support accurate tax and accounting lot accounting, PFinanc uses **First-In First-Out (FIFO)** lot depletion:

### Acquisition (BUY / SIP)
Each purchase creates an active lot:
$$\text{Lot}_i = \{ q_i, c_i, \text{date}_i \}$$
Where unit cost $c_i = (\text{Gross Amount} + \text{Brokerage Fees} + \text{Taxes}) / q_i$.

### Disposal (SELL / REDEMPTION)
A sale of quantity $Q_{\text{sold}}$ depletes lots in chronological FIFO order ($i = 1, 2, \dots$):
$$\text{Realized P\&L} = \sum_{\text{lots}} \Delta q_i \times (\text{Sale Price} - c_i) - \text{Sale Fees \& Taxes}$$

### Valuation (Holdings)
$$\text{Current Quantity} = \sum q_{\text{remaining}}$$
$$\text{Total Cost Basis} = \sum (q_{\text{remaining}} \times c_i)$$
$$\text{Average Cost} = \frac{\text{Total Cost Basis}}{\text{Current Quantity}}$$
$$\text{Current Market Value} = \text{Current Quantity} \times \text{Latest Price}$$
$$\text{Unrealized P\&L} = \text{Current Market Value} - \text{Total Cost Basis}$$
$$\text{Unrealized Return \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Unrealized P\&L}}{\text{Total Cost Basis}}\right) \times 100$$

---

## 4. XIRR Performance Calculation

Annualized internal rate of return (XIRR) is computed by solving for rate $r$ using the exact-day cash flows via the Newton-Raphson method:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{C_i}{(1 + r)^{\frac{d_i - d_0}{365.25}}} = 0$$

Where:
* $C_i < 0$ for investment purchases (BUY / SIP) on date $d_i$
* $C_i > 0$ for sales, redemptions, and dividend receipts on date $d_i$
* $C_{\text{final}} > 0$ represents the total current market value of the portfolio on today's date.
